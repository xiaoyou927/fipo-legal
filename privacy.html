Privacy Policy

Fipo is a book-page photo frame editor. You pick a frame, add a photo from the camera or your library, adjust the page, and render it into a picture you can keep on this device. Fipo does not create an account, does not upload your photos or notes to a Fipo server, and does not sell personal information.

Information stored on this device

Fipo keeps the following on the device where you use the app, in the app's local storage:

  • Photos you choose for a frame, including the draft photo and the finished page image.
  • The draft you have not rendered yet: frame style, filter, zoom, rotation, mark, rule, text, and drawing strokes.
  • Finished pages in your library, including the frame name and the date they were rendered.
  • Frame Credits, the local purchase ledger, and the product identifier of a verified purchase.
  • Support notes you type and save.
  • A flag that remembers whether the one-time notification permission prompt has already been shown.

This information stays in the app's sandbox. Clearing all data from Settings deletes framed pages, the draft, notes, purchase history, and remaining Frame Credits from this device. Deleting one page removes that page and its saved image. Fipo does not keep a copy elsewhere.

Camera

The camera is used only after you tap Take Photo. The system permission prompt appears at that moment, not when the app launches. If you deny access, you can still import a photo or type a line, and you can tap Take Photo again later. A photo you take is stored with the draft or the finished page on this device.

Photo library

Fipo asks to read your photo library only after you tap Import from Photos. It asks to add to your photo library only after you tap Save to Photos on a finished page. The app does not scan the library in the background. If you deny access, the page stays in the Fipo library and you can allow Photos in the system Settings and try the same action again.

Microphone and speech

The microphone and speech recognition are used only after you tap Voice input in the editor. Fipo asks for the microphone first, then speech recognition. Speech is recognized on the device. The resulting line is placed in the text field so you can edit it before you continue. The audio is not saved as a file and is not uploaded.

Notifications

The first time the Frames screen appears, Fipo may show the system notification permission prompt once. Fipo does not send local notifications or remote push notifications. It does not register for remote notifications, does not set a notification center delegate, and does not schedule alerts. Allowing or denying the prompt does not change framing, credits, or saving. The Settings screen only reads the current system status so you can see it. That read does not send a notification.

Purchases

Frame Credit packs are sold through Apple In-App Purchase. The pack names and prices shown in the app are Paper Pack at $0.99, Ink Pack at $2.99, Studio Pack at $9.99, Gallery Chest at $19.99, Atelier Bundle at $49.99, Archive Chest at $99.99, and Weekend Gift at $7.99. A credit is added only after Apple verifies the transaction. Canceling, a pending purchase, an unverified transaction, or a missing product does not change your balance. Fipo stores the transaction identifier on this device so the same transaction is not credited twice. Fipo does not include a Restore Purchases control.

Apple processes the payment. Fipo does not receive your full payment card number.

What Fipo does not do

  • Fipo does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not read the advertising identifier.
  • Fipo does not show ads and does not share data with an advertising network.
  • Fipo does not require an account, email address, or phone number.
  • Fipo does not upload photos, drafts, finished pages, notes, or credit balances to a Fipo server.
  • Fipo does not include a social feed, comments, or public profile.

Your choices

You can refuse the camera, photo library, microphone, speech recognition, or notification prompt. The rest of the app remains available. You can delete a single finished page from its detail screen. You can clear all local Fipo data from Settings. After a clear, the start screen appears again and the Frame Credit balance on that device is zero.

Children

Fipo is a general photo-framing tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated text will be published at this page. The copy shown inside Fipo is this page, opened in the app.

Contact

Support notes you write in the app stay on the device. They are not sent to a support team. This policy does not provide a separate mailbox because Fipo does not operate an account or a message service.
