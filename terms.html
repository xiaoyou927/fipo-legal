Frame Credits

A successful render uses 1 Frame Credit. The review screen shows the cost and your current balance before you render. If the balance is below 1, the render does not run and no credit is removed. Saving the same finished page to Photos again does not use another credit. Editing that page and rendering it again is a new render and uses 1 Frame Credit. If saving the finished file fails, the credit is not taken. Canceling while the render progress is on screen does not take a credit.

Credits are stored on this device. They are not an account balance and they are not transferred to another device.
